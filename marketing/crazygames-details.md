## Description
Build your street food empire! Start with a single burger truck in a parking lot and grow it into a city-wide snack business.

Serve hungry customers, unlock new recipes, hire cooks and upgrade your stands to earn more and more money, even while you're away. Expand from food trucks to a covered market, a fishing harbor, a sunny beach and a colorful fun fair, each with its own dishes and atmosphere.

Features:
- Relaxing idle gameplay: your stands keep earning money on their own
- 5 areas to unlock: food trucks, covered market, fishing harbor, beach and fun fair
- More than 35 recipes, from burgers and fries to sushi, lobster, crepes and cotton candy
- Hire cooks and buy upgrades: turbo grill, advertising, bigger waiting area
- Special events: fresh fish arrivals at the harbor, heat waves at the beach, magical nights at the fair
- Watch your little customers queue, order and sit down to eat in a colorful 3D city
- Progress is saved automatically

## Controls
Mouse:
- Left click: buy, upgrade and unlock
- Left click + drag: move around the city
- Right click + drag: rotate the camera
- Mouse wheel: zoom in and out

Keyboard:
- WASD or arrow keys: move the camera (hold Shift to go faster)

Touch screen:
- Tap: buy, upgrade and unlock
- Drag with one finger: move around
- Pinch / two fingers: zoom and rotate
